Selara is a prayer journal built for remembering — bring everything to God and look back on every answer.

Download on iOS
  1. Pray

    Bring everything.

    Log prayer requests by category — family, health, guidance, gratitude, and more. Add notes, scripture, and who you're praying for.

  2. AI-assisted

    Just speak it.

    Dictate your prayers out loud and let Selara's AI shape them into a journal entry — your words, your heart, no typing required.

  3. Journal

    Reflect deeply.

    Speak your heart and let Selara help you shape it into a journal entry. Your words, your prayers, your story with God.

  4. Guided

    Pray with a guide.

    Not sure where to start? Walk through Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving and Supplication, one step at a time.

  5. Remember

    See what He's done.

    Mark prayers as answered and look back on what God has done. Faith grows when you remember.

  6. Answered

    Notice the answers.

    Share good news and Selara finds the prayers it answers, so you can mark them answered and remember.

  7. Circles

    Pray together.

    Create a Circle to pray alongside friends, family, or your small group. Share requests and carry each other's burdens.

  8. Small group

    Bring the whole list.

    Paste the requests and praises you wrote down at small group. Selara splits them up for your Circle, and you review them before they post.

  9. Make it yours

    Pick your palette.

    Choose from nine color themes to dress Selara in a palette that fits your personality. Your journal, your look.
