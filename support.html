Help & Support

We're here
to help.

Have a question about Selara? Run into an issue? We'd love to hear from you.

Account & Sign In

If you're having trouble signing in, use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in screen to reset your password via email.

Prayer Circles

To invite someone to a Circle, open the circle and tap the share icon in the top right. Send them the invite link — they'll need a Selara account to join.

Data & Privacy

Your personal prayers are encrypted on your device before being stored. No one — including us — can read your private prayer journal. Content shared in Prayer Circles is also encrypted at rest and is readable only by circle members.

Delete Your Account

To permanently delete your account and all data, go to the Profile tab in the app and tap "Delete Account" at the bottom of the page.
